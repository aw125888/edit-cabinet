- Posts are owned by the user who catalogs them. Each post's metadata has "Cataloged by" rather than "owner" or "author" because archivists are not equivalent to creators. Someone cataloging an edit is not necessarily the creator of the edit. 

5. Page Divisions:
    The home page only displays buttons that lead to the Categorize and Search page. The other views (My Cabinet, My Edits) are universally available under the profile icon on the top right. This decision was made because actions should be distinguished from ownership. No personal account owns the search or categorize action but each personal account is linked to a personal cabinet and cataloged edits. 

- Owners can edit captions/delete their Posts. Owners can also add and remove tags (as long as minimum and maximum tag requirements are respected) This is managed by the Vue frontend and the Manage composition. 

- Deleting a post requires also cleaning up the associated Tagging + Collecting references.

- Cabinets/folder saves are private. No engagement analytics are made available. This is due to two reasons. One is that Edit Cabinet is an archive rather than a social media platform. It is implemented as a partner to video-hosting platforms such as Tiktok, Instagram, and Youtube. Likes, comments, and save counts are not available features within an archive even as they are available on partner video-hosting platform. Furthermore, edit owners aren't owners in the creative sense but rather in the catalog sense. Therefore, engagement analytics aren't necessarily personal to "owners" either.
